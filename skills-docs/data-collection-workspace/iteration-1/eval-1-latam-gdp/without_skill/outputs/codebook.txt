INDICATORS
----------
1. NY.GDP.PCAP.CD  ->  gdp_per_capita_current_usd
   Definition: GDP per capita is gross domestic product divided by midyear
   population. GDP is the sum of gross value added by all resident producers
   in the economy plus any product taxes and minus any subsidies not included
   in the value of the products. It is calculated without making deductions
   for depreciation of fabricated assets or for depletion and degradation of
   natural resources. Data are in current U.S. dollars.
   Unit: current US$

2. SP.POP.TOTL  ->  population
   Definition: Total population is based on the de facto definition of
   population, which counts all residents regardless of legal status or
   citizenship. The values shown are midyear estimates.
   Unit: persons

FILE DESCRIPTIONS
-----------------
1. latam_gdp_pcap_population_panel.csv  (main output, long format)
   Columns:
     - iso3                         : ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 country code
     - country_name                 : Country name (English)
     - year                         : Calendar year (integer)
     - gdp_per_capita_current_usd   : GDP per capita in current US$ (float)
     - population                   : Total population (integer)

2. latam_gdp_pcap_wide.csv  (GDP per capita, wide format)
   Rows: countries (iso3, country_name)
   Columns: gdp_pcap_2000, gdp_pcap_2001, ..., gdp_pcap_2023

3. latam_population_wide.csv  (Population, wide format)
   Rows: countries (iso3, country_name)
   Columns: pop_2000, pop_2001, ..., pop_2023

NOTES & CAVEATS
---------------
- Venezuela: GDP data may be unreliable for recent years due to economic
  crisis and reporting gaps. The World Bank stopped publishing some
  Venezuelan indicators around 2014-2018.
- Cuba: GDP data uses official exchange rates, which may not reflect
  purchasing power accurately.
- Missing values: Some country-year combinations may have NA values,
  particularly for Cuba and Venezuela in recent years.
- Currency: GDP per capita is in CURRENT US dollars (not constant/real).
  For time-series comparisons, consider using NY.GDP.PCAP.KD (constant
  2015 US$) or NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD (PPP, constant international $).

ALTERNATIVE INDICATORS (for future consideration)
--------------------------------------------------
- NY.GDP.PCAP.KD      : GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$)
- NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.CD   : GDP per capita, PPP (current international $)

- NY.GDP.MKTP.CD      : GDP (current US$) -- total, not per capita
